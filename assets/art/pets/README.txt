THESE ARE THE FULL PACK'S ANIMALS, SCALED DOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The frames here are the same drawings as in the paid pack, reduced by a whole
number with nearest-neighbour so the outlines stay hard - every animal by the
same factor, so a fox stays smaller than a bear and the four still work in one
scene together. Nothing was redrawn and nothing was softened; you are seeing
exactly the art you would get, at a smaller size.

The full pack has all nine rebuilt companions at 176 px in eight directions,
each with walk, attack and idle cycles, plus the owl and the light spirit:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
AI DISCLOSURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The pixel art was generated with PixelLab, an AI pixel art tool, and then
worked on by hand: gaits written per animal, cycles checked frame by frame,
rejected versions regenerated until they walked properly.

Scaling the frames down does not change how they were made, so this pack
carries the same disclosure as the paid one. Some buyers do not want
AI-generated art and some jams do not allow it - you should be able to decide
before you download, not after.
